When talent meets confidence, competition becomes a celebration of growth. The MBA First Year students of Francis Xavier Engineering College carried their classroom learning beyond the campus and into the vibrant arena of Comfiesta-26, organised by St. Xavier's College (Autonomous), Palayamkottai.

Eight Platforms. One Spirit of Excellence.

Comfiesta-26 gave students multiple opportunities to demonstrate creativity, communication, business thinking, visual storytelling, teamwork and confidence. The FXEC MBA contingent participated in eight events: Face Fiesta, Snap Saga, Dream to Deal, Sell & Spell, Design Rush, Fashionomics, Commerce Clash and Frame the Future.
