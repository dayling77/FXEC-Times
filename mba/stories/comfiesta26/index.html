First-Year MBA Students Turn Participation into Achievement

First-Year MBA students of Francis Xavier Engineering College turned participation in Comfiesta-26 into a showcase of ideas, teamwork, communication, creativity and determination.

FXEC Times · Special ReportSeptember 2026 · Palayamkottai

A competition can be measured in prizes, but its deeper value is measured in what students discover about themselves. At Comfiesta-26, MBA First Year students of Francis Xavier Engineering College stepped beyond the familiar boundaries of the classroom and entered a demanding arena of creativity, communication, business thinking, visual expression and teamwork.

Organised by St. Xavier's College (Autonomous), Palayamkottai, the event brought together a series of platforms that challenged participants to think quickly, present confidently and work effectively with others. For the FXEC MBA contingent, each event became an opportunity to transform preparation into performance.

“The real achievement is not simply winning a contest; it is discovering new confidence, creativity and the courage to take on the next challenge.”

Beyond the podium

What makes such experiences valuable is the learning that remains after the certificates and photographs are filed away. A presentation strengthens clarity. A team challenge teaches coordination. A creative brief demands originality. A competitive moment develops composure. Together, these experiences help students build the professional habits that cannot be acquired from textbooks alone.

For management students in particular, the ability to communicate an idea, understand an audience, collaborate under pressure and convert a concept into an outcome is central to professional life. Comfiesta-26 provided a practical setting in which those capabilities could be tested, refined and celebrated.

Why It Matters

Participation creates exposure. Exposure builds confidence. Confidence encourages students to take on bigger challenges.

A story that continues beyond the event.

Comfiesta-26 is a reminder that meaningful achievement grows from participation, preparation and the courage to be seen. For the MBA First Year students of FXEC, the experience adds another chapter to a larger journey — learning to think, communicate, collaborate and lead with confidence.
