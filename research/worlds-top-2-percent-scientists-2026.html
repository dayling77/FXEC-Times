Two FXEC Academicians Listed Among the World’s Top 2% Scientists – 2026

A proud moment for Francis Xavier Engineering College as two faculty members earn recognition in a distinguished global listing of scientific researchers.

FXEC Times Editorial Desk   |   Tirunelveli, Tamil Nadu

TIRUNELVELI: Francis Xavier Engineering College (FXEC), an autonomous institution in Tirunelveli, takes pride in the distinction of having two of its faculty members listed among the World’s Top 2% Scientists – 2026, in a ranking associated with Stanford University researchers and Elsevier’s Scopus data.

Dr. V. VelmuruganPrincipal and Professor
Department of Mechanical Engineering
Dr. Y. Harold RobinsonProfessor
Department of Computer Science and Engineering

The recognition marks a significant academic milestone for the institution and celebrates the role of sustained research, scholarly contributions and innovation in advancing knowledge.

“A proud milestone reflecting FXEC’s commitment to research, innovation and academic excellence.”

The achievement reflects the college’s continuing efforts to nurture a strong research culture, encourage interdisciplinary collaboration and promote excellence in engineering and technology education. Research and knowledge creation remain central to addressing emerging challenges and contributing to scientific and technological advancement.

Francis Xavier Engineering College congratulates Dr. V. Velmurugan and Dr. Y. Harold Robinson on this distinction and wishes them continued success in their academic and research endeavours.
