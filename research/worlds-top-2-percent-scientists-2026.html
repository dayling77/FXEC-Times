Two FXEC Academicians Listed Among the World’s Top 2% Scientists – 2026

FXEC Times · Francis Xavier Engineering College, Tirunelveli

TIRUNELVELI, TAMIL NADU: Francis Xavier Engineering College (FXEC), an autonomous institution in Tirunelveli, takes pride in the distinction of having two of its faculty members listed among the World’s Top 2% Scientists – 2026, in a ranking associated with Stanford University researchers and Elsevier’s Scopus data.

Dr. V. Velmurugan
Principal and Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering

Dr. Y. Harold Robinson
Professor, Department of Computer Science and Engineering

This recognition celebrates research contributions, scholarly impact and the pursuit of academic excellence.

The inclusion of both academicians in this distinguished list marks a significant academic achievement for the institution and highlights the importance of scientific research, scholarly contributions and innovation in advancing knowledge.

The achievement also reflects FXEC’s commitment to fostering a strong research culture, encouraging interdisciplinary collaboration and promoting excellence in engineering and technology education.

The institution continues to support research and knowledge creation to address emerging challenges and contribute to scientific and technological advancement.

Francis Xavier Engineering College congratulates Dr. V. Velmurugan and Dr. Y. Harold Robinson on this achievement and wishes them continued success in their academic and research endeavours.
