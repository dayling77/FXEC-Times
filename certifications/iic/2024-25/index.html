Francis Xavier Engineering College Earns 4-Star IIC Rating for the Sixth Time

Francis Xavier Engineering College (FXEC) has earned a 4-Star Rating from the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), Ministry of Education, Government of India, for the sixth time.

The national recognition acknowledges the Institution's Innovation Council established at FXEC for undertaking activities prescribed by the Innovation Cell, Ministry of Education, Government of India, to promote innovation and start-up development on campus during the IIC Calendar Year 2024–25.

4-STAR RATING
A national recognition for activities undertaken to promote Innovation and Start-up in the campus during the IIC Calendar Year 2024–25.

Sustained Commitment to Innovation

This recognition reflects FXEC's continued efforts to create opportunities for students to ideate, innovate, develop solutions and explore entrepreneurship. Through innovation-focused activities and initiatives, the institution continues to encourage students to move from ideas to practical solutions with meaningful impact.

The sixth-time recognition from the Institution's Innovation Council is a significant national acknowledgement of the institution's sustained participation in the innovation and start-up ecosystem promoted by the Ministry of Education.
