Institutional Recognition • IIC

Sixth-Time IIC 4-Star Rating

Francis Xavier Engineering College has earned a 4-Star Rating from the Institution's Innovation Council (IIC), Ministry of Education, Government of India, for the sixth time.

The recognition is for activities undertaken during the IIC Calendar Year 2024–25, promoting innovation and start-up development on campus.

Recognition: IIC 4-Star Rating
Calendar Year: 2024–25
Recognition: Sixth-Time Recipient
Certificate Issued: 16 April 2026
